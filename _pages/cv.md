# Projects

**Polymarket Research Stack**

- [Data Engine](https://github.com/SuperPolyrio/market-data). Reconstructs the lifecycle from market creation and fills through oracle resolution.
- [Execution Simulator](https://github.com/SuperPolyrio/paper-trading). Simulates execution, liquidity, order-book dynamics, and paper-to-live fidelity.
- [Replay Lab](https://github.com/SuperPolyrio/backtest-lab). Replays fill-level, trade-only, and L2 history with oracle-aware settlement.

**[Polymarket Smart Money](https://github.com/SuperPolyrio/smart-money)**

- Evidence-based research on Polymarket wallets, from historical qualification and confirmed trades to verified market evidence.

**PolyMonitor**

- Public interface for Polymarket prices, fills, oracle events, and lifecycle-aligned market data. [Project](https://www.polymonitor.club/) · [Code](https://github.com/virusLuke3/polymonitor)
